    Keyboard & tips

    Type or paste a complete expression. Multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction. Parentheses work in both modes: 2*(3+4) or 2(3+4).

    Use a decimal point or comma. Fractions: 1/2 + 3/4. Mixed numbers: 2 1/3 or -1 1/2. Scientific notation: 1e-6.

    % divides by 100: 200*10% = 20. To add 10%, use 200*(1+10%) = 220.

    Enter / = calculate · Esc clear · Backspace delete · F9 change sign.

    Ctrl/Cmd+C copies selected text, or the result when nothing is selected. Ctrl/Cmd+V pastes at the cursor. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes edits; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redoes them.

    ↑ / ↓ recall expressions. A digit after = starts fresh; an operator continues with the exact result. Pressing = again keeps that result.

    ≈ marks a decimal rounded to 16 significant digits. Fraction and mixed views stay exact. Copy follows the selected result format. History is saved in this browser when storage is available.
